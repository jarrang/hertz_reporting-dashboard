Campaign detail

How to read this report

This view covers completed, one-off scheduled sends. Campaign date filters use the send date, with engagement accumulated up to each summary's last recalculation. Ongoing triggered sends are outside this view.

Periods and comparisons
7, 30 or 90 complete days, ending on the report's through-date. Comparisons use the immediately preceding period of equal length. Newer campaigns have had less time to collect engagement. “pp” means percentage points.
Delivery rate
Delivered ÷ sent. Delivery indicates that a message was not recorded as bounced; it does not confirm inbox placement.
Click rate and open rate
Unique clicks ÷ delivered, and unique opens ÷ delivered. Period rates use summed counts, never averages of job rates. Unique counts are unique within each job, not deduplicated people across the whole period. Automated activity can affect tracking; Apple Mail privacy features can inflate opens.
Unsubscribe and complaint rates
Unsubscribes ÷ delivered and complaints ÷ delivered. Bounce rate uses bounces ÷ sent. A missing denominator displays as “N/A”.
Recent campaigns and exports
The table contains a bounded recent-job list. Search and sort apply only to that loaded list. Export includes all matching loaded rows across every table page. Summary cards and the chart use all matching jobs in the loaded payload before the table cap. The report appears only after all campaign batches for the reporting and comparison periods have loaded. Incomplete retrieval displays an error instead of partial totals.
Audience and journeys
These are All EMEA latest-snapshot views and do not change with the campaign period. They are hidden while a region or send-stage filter is active. Active status alone is not proof of campaign eligibility or consent. Segments can overlap, so their sizes should not be added together. Journey status is per version as recorded in the extract.
Region and send stage
Region comes from the exact EUxx token in EmailName. PNT identifies Parent sends. Unknown codes stay unclassified and remain included in All regions / All stages. RM identifies Remail sends. Current and recognised legacy generator structures are supported. Region identifies the campaign market, not verified recipient residence. A regional URL selects a view; it does not restrict access to other regions.
Loading and freshness
Campaign summaries load first. Audience snapshots and link details load only when requested, and successful results are reused for this page visit. The retrieved time records when the payload was prepared; it does not mean that the underlying data was recalculated then. Campaign freshness uses LastRecalculated where supplied. Unknown dates remain unverified. Link details can advance after the main report was loaded.
